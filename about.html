GET TO KNOW MAVUTIHUB

More Than Clubs. A Place to Belong.

MavutiHub is a student-focused platform designed to make it easier for Vaal University of Technology students to discover clubs, societies and communities that reflect their interests, passions and ambitions.

Discover Our Story
One Campus. Endless Connections.
THE IDEA BEHIND MAVUTIHUB

Campus Life Is Better Together.

Great university experiences aren't built in classrooms alone. They're built through friendships, shared interests and the communities we become part of.

But discovering the right clubs and societies isn't always easy. Students may miss opportunities simply because they don't know what's available or where to begin.

That's why we created MavutiHub: one place to explore student communities, discover opportunities and find your people.
